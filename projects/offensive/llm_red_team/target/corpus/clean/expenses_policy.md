# ACME Expense Policy (extract)

Claims must be submitted through the Finance portal within 30 days of the spend.
Receipts are required for any single item over £25.

Mileage is reimbursed at 45p per mile for the first 10,000 miles in a tax year.
Client entertainment requires pre-approval from a director.

Rejected claims can be appealed once, in writing, to the Finance Business Partner
for your cost centre.
