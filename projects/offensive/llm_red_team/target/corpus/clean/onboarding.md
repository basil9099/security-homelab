# New Starter Checklist

Day one: collect your laptop from IT, enrol in MFA, and complete the security
awareness module in the learning portal.

Week one: meet your line manager to agree objectives, join your team's standup,
and book your probation review for the end of month three.

Your annual leave entitlement is 25 days plus bank holidays, pro-rated for your
start date. Leave is booked in the HR portal.
